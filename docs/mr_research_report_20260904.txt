## CRITIC FIX APPLIED

Before fix (line 136 of critic.py):
  ok_oos = mo.get("mean_net", -1) > 0 and (mo.get("n") or 0) >= min_ev

After fix:
  ok_oos = (mo.get("mean_net", -1) > 0
            and (mo.get("n") or 0) >= min_ev
            and (mo.get("t_stat") or 0) >= _R["min_t_stat"])

The old gate accepted EV>0 with any t-stat. A result with
EV=+0.00003 and t=0.06 passed — which is statistically noise.

The new gate requires t >= 2.0, ensuring OOS results are
statistically significant, not just numerically positive.
